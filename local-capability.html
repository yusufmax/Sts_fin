Local Capability Development

Building Local Capability Through Global Technology.

Advanced technology creates lasting value when people have the knowledge and skills to use, maintain and develop it.

The approach

Global expertise, grounded locally.

Strategic Security Systems works to connect international technology expertise with local requirements and technical talent in Uzbekistan.

Through cooperation with technology partners, we seek to support practical skills, technical knowledge and sustainable support capabilities. The scope of any training or localization activity depends on specific partner and project agreements.

Development directions

Practical capability is built together.

01

Knowledge & Technical Training

We seek to facilitate access to technical expertise and training opportunities for local personnel.

02

Integration & Implementation

We coordinate technical activities to bring equipment, software and supporting components into a functional solution.

03

Maintenance & Technical Support

We support arrangements for technical assistance, maintenance and lifecycle support with relevant technology partners.

04

Local Industry Cooperation

We seek opportunities to involve qualified local companies and specialists in implementation and support.

Our long-term objective

Turn access to international technology into practical knowledge, stronger local expertise and sustainable technical capability in Uzbekistan.
